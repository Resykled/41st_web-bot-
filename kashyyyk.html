Kashyyyk Tactical Map

Planet Information

Kashyyyk is a tropical jungle planet, home to the Wookiees. The planet's surface is covered by wroshyr trees that can grow to several kilometers in height.

Strategic Points

Wookiee settlements are primarily located in the higher elevation areas, connected by an intricate network of bridges and platforms.
